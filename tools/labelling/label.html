    Keyboard shortcuts

    Shortcuts work unless you are typing in a field.

    J or ↓ / K or ↑Next / previous region
    SpacePlay or stop the selection, the selected region, or 30 s from the cursor
    Shift+SpacePlay the selected region with 5 s either side
    Y / X / UMark snoring (and go to the next region) / not snoring (and go to the next) / undecided
    A / SStart 1 s earlier / later (with Shift: 5 s)
    D / FEnd 1 s earlier / later (with Shift: 5 s)
    NAdd a region from the selection, or 20 s from the cursor
    DelDelete an added region (proposed regions are marked not snoring instead)
    ← / →Pan the detail view
    + / -Zoom the detail view in / out
