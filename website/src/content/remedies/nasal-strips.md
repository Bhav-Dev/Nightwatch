question: Do nasal strips help with snoring?

steps:
  - Put the strip across the bridge of the nose, just above the flare of the nostrils, on clean dry skin.
  - They are cheap and easy to test, so give them at least a week of nights before you decide.
caution: See a GP if your nose is blocked most of the time.

Adhesive strips that sit across the bridge of the nose and gently lift the sides of the nostrils, which can make breathing through the nose easier. They tend to help most when snoring starts in the nose, for example with congestion or narrow nostrils.
