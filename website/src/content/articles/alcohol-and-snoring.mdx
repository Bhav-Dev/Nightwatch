Alcohol is near the top of almost every list of snoring advice. It relaxes the muscles of the throat, and it is one of the most common reasons people give for a loud night. What is less clear is how much difference a drink or two makes on an ordinary night, and whether the timing matters.

We compared nights where people logged drinks in the evening with their own nights without. Comparing each person with themselves takes out differences between people, such as age or how loudly they snore anyway.
