# Phone test plan

Everything built since 16 October 2026 is tested in code but has not run on a phone. This is the list to work through on the Samsung S24 Ultra (and later an iPhone), with the newest preview build installed. Tick items in [status.md](status.md) as they pass, and note anything odd with the time it happened so the night can be checked.

## 1. A 20-minute daytime test

Most of This Morning can be checked without waiting for a night.

1. Pick a remedy and a factor on Tonight, turn sleep sounds off, and start recording. Put the phone on the bed or a table next to you and lock it.
2. At about 5 minutes, play some snoring from another device (any snoring video) for 2 to 3 minutes, close to the phone.
3. Right after it stops, rustle the duvet or a pillow near the phone a few times over a minute, as if turning over.
4. At about 10 minutes, unlock the phone, open SnoreHive, look at it for a minute, then lock it again.
5. At about 13 minutes, rustle again three times within a couple of minutes.
6. At 20 minutes, stop.

Then on This Morning check:

- the score sits in the honey hexagon, with the label and the change against your average beside it;
- the timeline shows the snoring; dragging along it shows the time, and letting go plays from there; previous and next step through the moments;
- under the timeline: **Movement** shows ticks around steps 3 and 5, with a shaded restless stretch at step 5; **Possibly awake** shows a bar at step 4;
- tapping a lane plays the sound from that moment;
- the summary (Insight) says "Snoring stopped after we heard you turn over at …" if the quiet after step 3 lasted 15 minutes. In a 20-minute test it will not, so expect it on a real night;
- the Snoring tick on a snoring moment: untick it and the score, time snoring and timeline change, "Was N before your answers" appears, and Undo puts it back;
- last night's log shows as honeycomb cells with the names underneath; tapping it opens the picker.

Movement in the first and last 2 minutes is ignored on purpose, and phone use in the first 2 and last 3 minutes too.

## 2. Install, name and look

- The app is called SnoreHive, with the honeycomb cell icon. Long-press the home screen to try other icon shapes, and turn on themed icons.
- Opening the app shows the splash (the honeycomb cell on the night colour).
- The welcome screen (after "Delete all data" in Settings) shows the logo and "SnoreHive".
- Buttons and highlights are honey.

## 3. Tonight

- Remedies and factors show as honeycomb cells (remedies honey, factors outlined), with alcohol's units as a badge and a "+" cell. More than fit in a row wrap to a shifted second row.
- The picker shows each item's icon, name and tick.
- Sleep sounds: preview each colour; the chosen timer fades the sound out.
- Wake-up alarm: with your usual alarm set in Samsung Clock, the row shows it; tapping opens Clock. With no alarm for the morning, "Set" adds one to Clock, and Tonight then shows it.

## 4. A real night

- Plug the phone in, start recording, sleep.
- With an alarm set: the recording stops about 30 seconds before it rings, and This Morning is ready.
- In the morning: the lanes show movement through the night; if you got up or checked the phone, Possibly awake shows it.
- With a Galaxy Watch connected through Health Connect: the Sleep stages lane shows deep, light, REM and awake (Insight).
- The night recap notification arrives at 12:30 (or the time chosen in Settings) and opens This Morning.

## 5. The rest

- **Trends:** "Your hive of nights" shows one cell per day, and tapping a night opens it. Create the doctor's report and open the PDF: the honey and indigo design, the chart and the tables.
- **Remedies tab:** each remedy has its icon. Community results need sign-in and the server, so expect "needs a connection" in this build.
- **Interruption:** start a recording, then take a phone call. The night should end as partial, with "Recording stopped at …".
- **Battery (later):** a night unplugged, noting the percentage before and after; the goal is under 8% for 8 hours.

## iPhone

Once an iPhone build exists, repeat sections 1 to 5. The wake-up alarm is Android only for now, so Tonight hides it on iPhone.
